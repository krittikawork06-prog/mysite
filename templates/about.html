WHO WE ARE

More Than Food,
It's an Experience

Founded in 2012, Sarab began with a simple idea: create delicious food that brings people together. What started as a small neighbourhood restaurant has grown into a place where families, friends and food lovers come together to enjoy memorable meals.

From our kitchen to your table, we focus on bold flavours, quality ingredients and friendly service. Every dish is prepared with care so that every visit feels special.
